How Relief works

  1. Someone submits a billA person in hardship uploads a real bill they cannot pay.
  2. A masjid verifies itA local committee checks the bill and confirms whether zakat may be used.
  3. Donors pay itPart or all of it, as sadaqah or zakat, with Gift Aid if they pay UK tax.
  4. The biller is paid directlyNo cash changes hands, and the donor gets a receipt.

Recipients stay anonymous. All zakat reaches the bill. Gift Aid reclaimed from HMRC funds more bills. Running costs come from optional tips. Debts are paid without interest.
